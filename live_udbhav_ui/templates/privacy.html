Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 8, 2026

Overview

This site is a personal dashboard with optional Google sign-in. We collect only the information needed to operate the features you use, such as diary entries, journal data, and calendar events.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: your Google account email and name (if you sign in with Google).
  • Dashboard content: entries you create (diary, strategies, journal, links).
  • Photos you upload for diary entries.
  • Calendar data you grant access to (primary calendar only).

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and personalize your dashboard.
  • To display and organize your content.
  • To sync calendar events you choose to connect.
  • To maintain backups you request.

Data Storage

Data is stored on the server hosting Udbhav.uk. Backups may be transferred to a private Windows machine owned by the operator. We do not sell your data.

Third-Party Services

We use Google OAuth and the Google Calendar API to connect your account when you request it. Google’s own privacy terms also apply.

Your Choices

  • You can disconnect Google at any time from within the dashboard.
  • You can request deletion of your stored data by contacting the operator.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the site operator.

This is a personal dashboard and not intended for regulated or sensitive medical, legal, or financial data.
